# Promotion Rules

These rules help affiliates and creators promote DolOffer without misleading
users or damaging partner trust.

## Required

- Disclose affiliate, sponsored, or paid relationships clearly.
- Link to [doloffer.com](https://doloffer.com/) as the source of truth.
- Verify product availability, pricing, refund policy, and region support before
  publishing.
- Explain the product format when relevant: shared slot, recharge, private
  account, family group, or invitation.
- Use review dates for price, plan, and availability claims.

## Not Allowed

- Fake reviews, fake traffic, bot clicks, self-referrals, or simulated purchases.
- Coupon-only spam or uploading personal affiliate links and promo codes to
  third-party coupon websites.
- Impersonating DolOffer, third-party platforms, support staff, or payment
  providers.
- Claiming official platform partnership without approved wording.
- Promising permanent access, unlimited use, guaranteed availability, or
  risk-free purchases.
- Encouraging refund abuse, account abuse, or platform-rule evasion.
- Asking users to send passwords, verification codes, payment credentials, or
  sensitive data to unknown third parties.

## Recommended Disclosures

For reviews:

```text
This review may include a DolOffer affiliate link or promo code. I may receive a
commission or other benefit if you purchase through it. Prices, availability,
refund rules, and supported regions can change, so check doloffer.com before
ordering.
```

For short social posts:

```text
Affiliate disclosure: this DolOffer link/code may earn me a commission. Verify
current product details at doloffer.com.
```

## Claims To Verify Before Publishing

- Starting price or discount amount.
- Supported country or region.
- Product delivery method.
- Whether the product is shared, private, recharge, or family-group based.
- Refund eligibility and processing timing.
- Renewal availability.
- Usage limits for AI products or family-plan subscriptions.
