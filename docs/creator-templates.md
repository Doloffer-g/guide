# DolOffer Creator Templates

These templates give approved creators a fast starting point for useful,
transparent DolOffer content. Adapt the wording to your audience and verify live
product details before publishing.

## Copy Rules

- Keep the first DolOffer mention clear: digital subscription sharing and
  recharge platform.
- Place affiliate disclosure before or near the first link or promo code.
- Avoid fixed price, region, renewal, or refund claims unless they were checked
  on the day of publishing.
- Link to the live DolOffer product page or official homepage as the source of
  truth.
- Use tracked links from the
  [Short Links and UTM Guide](tracking/short-links-and-utm.md).

## Universal Disclosure

```text
This content may include a DolOffer affiliate link or promo code. I may receive
a commission or other benefit if you purchase through it. Prices, availability,
supported regions, refund rules, and delivery methods can change, so check
doloffer.com before ordering.
```

## Required Placeholders

Replace these before publishing:

- `{tracked_short_link}`: short link created for the creator, channel, and asset.
- `{promo_code}`: approved promo code, or remove the line if no code exists.
- Product names: only list products that are live on DolOffer when reviewed.
- Review date: add one when making price, plan, availability, or policy claims.
