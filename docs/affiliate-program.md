# DolOffer Affiliate Program

DolOffer's affiliate program is designed for creators, review sites, community
owners, newsletter writers, and product educators who can introduce DolOffer to
relevant audiences.

## How It Works

1. Apply through the DolOffer website.
2. After approval, receive a unique affiliate link or promo code.
3. Share the link or code in eligible content channels.
4. DolOffer tracks registrations, purchases, and attributed orders.
5. Valid commissions become available after the applicable review and refund
   window.

The website includes an affiliate dashboard with registration counts, order
counts, commission status, promo code information, payout records, and a
partnership manager section.

## Public Commission Notes

Public DolOffer materials describe commission opportunities of up to 15% for
qualified affiliates. Actual rates may depend on product type, sales volume,
channel quality, refund risk, and partner agreement.

Use the affiliate dashboard or written partner agreement as the source of truth
for:

- commission rate;
- attribution rules;
- refund hold period;
- payout threshold;
- payout method;
- renewal commission eligibility.

Do not publish fixed commission, payout, or settlement promises unless DolOffer
has confirmed the wording for your channel.

## Recommended Promotion Channels

- YouTube and short-form video reviews.
- Blogs, newsletters, and comparison articles.
- Community groups where subscription tools are relevant.
- Tutorials for ChatGPT, YouTube Premium, Spotify, iCloud, Office 365, Notion,
  Duolingo, and similar products.
- Product roundups that explain tradeoffs, limitations, and refund expectations.

## Not Supported

DolOffer does not support low-quality coupon spam or promotion that harms
affiliate transparency. Avoid:

- posting affiliate links or codes on coupon-only websites;
- self-referrals or fake purchases;
- fake traffic, paid bot clicks, or misleading conversion activity;
- impersonating DolOffer or third-party platform brands;
- hiding the fact that your content is sponsored or affiliate-linked.
