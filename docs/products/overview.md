## Publishing Notes

- Verify live availability before publishing a product claim.
- Do not publish exact pricing without a review date.
- Mention whether the product is shared, private, recharge, or family-group
  based.
- Explain limitations honestly: region, renewal, account type, refund rules, and
  usage limits may differ by product.
- Use [Promotion Rules](../policies/promotion-rules.md) before adding links or
  promo codes.
