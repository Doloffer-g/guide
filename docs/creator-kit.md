# DolOffer Creator Kit

This creator kit gives partners a clean starting point for reviews, tutorials,
short videos, newsletters, community posts, and product explainers.

## Core Message

DolOffer helps users access selected streaming, AI, music, cloud, productivity,
and learning subscriptions through shared plans, recharge options, private
accounts, and family or group invitations.

Use plain wording:

> DolOffer is a digital subscription sharing and recharge platform. It offers
> selected AI tools, streaming services, music apps, cloud storage, productivity
> tools, and learning subscriptions with support, renewal, and refund policies
> shown on the website.

## Key Selling Points

- Broad product catalog across AI, streaming, music, productivity, cloud, and
  learning.
- Shared-plan savings compared with some individual official subscriptions.
- Affiliate links and promo codes for approved partners.
- Order, renewal, refund, and support flows inside the website.
- Multilingual positioning for English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese,
  and Korean audiences.

```text
productivity apps at the same time.

DolOffer is a digital subscription sharing and recharge platform. It lists
products such as ChatGPT, YouTube Premium, Spotify, Apple Music, iCloud, Office

Value:
Before buying, check the product type, supported region, renewal rule, refund
policy, and whether it is a shared slot, recharge, private account, or family
group invitation.

availability can change, so verify the live DolOffer page before ordering.
```

## Required Disclosure Language

Use a clear disclosure near the first link or call to action:

> This post may contain a DolOffer affiliate link or promo code. I may receive a
> commission or other benefit if you purchase through it. Check doloffer.com for
> current prices, product availability, refund rules, and terms.

## Avoid These Claims

- "Guaranteed permanent access"
- "Unlimited usage"
- "Official partner of every listed platform"
- "No refund restrictions"
- "Works in every region"
- "Lowest price on the internet"
- "No risk"

Use balanced wording that includes product limits, account type, supported
region, and refund expectations.
